Our Expertise

Calibration Capabilities

Comprehensive metrology solutions spanning four major disciplines, backed by NABL accreditation and cutting-edge equipment.

Mechanical

Precision calibration for pressure gauges, torque wrenches, weighing scales, and mass standards with traceable accuracy.

Thermal

Reliable temperature mapping and calibration for thermometers, ovens, furnaces, RTD sensors, and thermocouples.

Electro-Technical

Accurate measurement testing for multimeters, clamp meters, power supplies, and insulation testers.

Dimensional

Verification for micrometers, vernier calipers, gauge blocks, and dial gauges using advanced master equipment.

How It Works

Our Calibration Process

A streamlined, transparent workflow designed to deliver precision results with minimal downtime for your operations.

1
Step 01

Instrument Receipt & Inspection

Your instruments are received, logged, and thoroughly inspected for condition assessment and calibration readiness.

2
Step 02

Precision Calibration

Expert technicians perform calibration using traceable reference standards in our climate-controlled, NABL-accredited lab.

3
Step 03

Certificate Generation

Detailed calibration certificates are generated with measurement data, uncertainty analysis, and full traceability information.

4
Step 04

Delivery & Support

Calibrated instruments and certificates are delivered promptly, with ongoing support for re-calibration scheduling.
